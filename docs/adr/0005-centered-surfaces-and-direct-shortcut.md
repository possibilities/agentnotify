# 0005 — Centered presentation and direct Complete All shortcut

Accepted 2026-09-29. Supersedes the anchor and pointer placement of [0002](0002-stable-menu-bar-anchor.md) and [0003 native panel](0003-native-status-panel.md), and the confirmed global-shortcut behavior of [0004](0004-visible-completion-undo.md). Their pinning, dismissal, and exact Undo semantics remain in force.

Present arrivals at the visible top center of the status item's display, regardless of the icon's horizontal location. Open the inbox at the same display's horizontal center with its center slightly above the visible midpoint, clamped inside the screen. The popover has no triangle or reserved strip; pinned and unpinned content occupy the same rectangle. Keep manually dragged placement through pin changes and recenter only on a fresh unpinned opening. This removes a misleading visual connection to the icon while keeping surfaces predictable on multiple or auto-hidden displays.

The global shortcut completes all active Inbox notifications immediately, even when a filter is selected. It never opens a confirmation sheet or opens a hidden inbox. After at least one successful completion it dismisses a visible inbox; the existing exact-batch Undo remains in its bottom bar for 60 seconds and is available on reopening. An empty or wholly failed completion does not dismiss the inbox. Use the existing shared status batch contract so prompts are closed durably before any external effect; Undo cannot reverse a response or callback. The header checkmark remains scoped to the visible active notifications.
