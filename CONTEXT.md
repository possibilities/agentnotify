# Glossary

## Notification record

A durable account of a requested notification and its delivery attributes.

Avoid: “notification” when the distinction between the record and its visible presentation matters.

## Dismiss

Set a Notification record’s dismissal state without ending its visible presentation.

Avoid: “delete,” “remove,” or “close.”

## Close

Ask for a visible notification presentation to end without changing the corresponding Notification record’s dismissal state.

Avoid: “dismiss” or “delete.”

## Delivery method

The mechanism that presents a notification to a destination.

Avoid: “channel” when referring to the concrete delivery mechanism.
